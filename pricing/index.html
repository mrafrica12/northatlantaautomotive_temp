What things cost here

"Our technicians are salaried, not commissioned" is a claim. Published pricing is the proof. These are real ranges for the work we do most often.

How to read this

These are ranges, not quotes. Where your vehicle lands depends on the things named in the last column — engine size, parts grade, and what we find once it's on the lift. You'll see photographs and a firm price before you approve anything.

If your estimate comes back above these ranges, we'll show you the photos and explain why before you approve anything. Sometimes there's a real reason — a seized caliper, a sheared bolt, a part that's only available from the dealer. You should never find that out from the invoice.

Why we publish this at all

Almost no independent shop posts prices, and the reason usually given is that every car is different. That's true, and it's why these are ranges. But it isn't a reason to make you call three shops to find out whether you're being taken advantage of.

Our technicians earn the same whether you buy the repair or not. Publishing what things cost is how we make that checkable rather than just something we say on a website.

What's not on this list

Engine and transmission work, and anything that starts as a symptom rather than a defined job. We won't put a range on those, because an honest range would be so wide it'd be useless. Those get diagnosed first, then quoted — with photographs — before any work begins.
