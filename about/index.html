About North Atlanta Automotive

Who we are

An independent, locally owned repair shop at 7095 Jimmy Carter Blvd in Peachtree Corners, serving drivers here and in Norcross since 2018. Not a dealership and not a franchise — the same people work on your car each time you come in.

Our technicians are salaried, not commissioned

This is the most important sentence on our website, and it's worth explaining why.

In much of this industry, technicians and service advisors are paid on what they sell. That doesn't make them dishonest — but it does mean that when someone looks at your car, there is a structural reason to find more work. The incentive sits in the pay structure, not in the person.

Ours are salaried. They earn the same whether you approve the repair or decline it. When we tell you your brake pads have another year in them, nobody here loses money by saying so.

Our story

Elias grew up working in his father's repair shop in Chamblee, and opened this one in 2018. Several of our customers have been coming to us — and to the people here before that — for decades. One of our Google reviews is from a customer of over thirty years.

What we're not going to do

No scare tactics. No inflated estimates. No list of urgent findings discovered during a routine oil change that we can't show you. If we recommend something, you'll see a photograph of why.

What we're still working on
