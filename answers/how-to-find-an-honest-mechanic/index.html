How to Find an Honest Mechanic

The short answer

Ask three questions: can you show me the problem, are your technicians paid commission, and what is your warranty in writing. A shop that photographs the failed part, pays technicians a salary, and publishes its warranty has removed its own incentives to oversell. Everything else is a claim.

Ask to see the problem

Any shop can show you the worn pad, the leaking gasket, the split boot. A shop that cannot produce evidence, or is irritated by being asked, is telling you something. Photographs sent to your phone before approval are the modern version of being walked into the bay.

Ask how the technicians are paid

This is the question almost nobody asks, and it is the most revealing one. If technicians earn commission on what they sell, the incentive to find extra work is structural rather than personal. Our technicians are salaried, which is why we are comfortable putting the question in your hands.

Ask for the warranty in writing

A shop confident in its work will state its parts and labour warranty plainly, including what happens if the car comes back. A vague answer here is worth more attention than a slightly higher quote.

Read the negative reviews, not the positive ones

Every shop has bad weeks. What matters is the pattern — and how the shop responds. A calm, specific reply to a complaint tells you more about how you will be treated when something goes wrong than fifty five-star reviews tell you about the good days.

Warning signs worth taking seriously

Pressure to decide immediately. A refusal to give a written estimate before work begins. Work performed that you did not approve. And the classic: a long list of urgent additional findings discovered during a routine oil change, none of which you can be shown.

Related questions

Should I get a second opinion?

On any large repair, yes, and a good shop will not be offended. We would rather you were confident than compliant.

Is a cheaper quote always worse?

No, but compare what is actually being fitted. Pads without rotors that should have been replaced is a cheaper quote for a different job.
