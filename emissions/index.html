Georgia emissions testing in Gwinnett County

The short answer

For 2026 registration, petrol cars and light trucks from 2002–2023 under 8,500 lbs GVWR must pass an emissions test in Gwinnett County. Testing is capped at $25 and you get one free retest at the original station within 30 calendar days. A lit check engine light is an automatic failure.

Do I need a test this year?

Must be testedPetrol cars and light trucks, model years 2002–2023, 8,500 lbs GVWR or less
Exempt — too new2024, 2025 and 2026 model years (the three most recent)
Exempt — too old2001 and older model years (25 years and older)
Also exemptDiesel vehicles, motorcycles, RVs and motor homes

We're not a testing station — and that's deliberate

Testing is a commodity capped at $25 and available all over Gwinnett. What most drivers actually struggle to find is a shop that can fix the reason they failed, document it properly, and get them back through before the retest window closes.

That's the part we do.

Why your registration is the real deadline

Georgia ties emissions testing to vehicle registration, and renewal is tied to your birthday. Until you pass — or qualify for a repair waiver — the tag office won't process your renewal. The test isn't the deadline; your registration date is.

The check engine light problem

Georgia's programme reads your car's computer rather than the tailpipe on most vehicles. A lit check engine light fails automatically. So does a car with incomplete readiness monitors — which is exactly what you get if you clear the code or disconnect the battery shortly before testing.

How much does the emissions test cost?

Georgia caps it at $25, and each station sets its own price within that range. That fee covers the initial test and one free retest if you fail.

How long is the free retest valid?

30 calendar days from the initial test, at the original station only. Take it somewhere else and you pay again.

What if repairs don't get me a pass?

Georgia has a repair waiver requiring $1,176 in qualifying repairs for 2026 registration, plus two failed tests. See our repair waiver page.

Do you perform the test here?

No. We're a repair shop, not a testing station. We fix the reason you failed and make sure you're ready before you use your free retest.
