The Georgia Emissions Repair Waiver, Explained

The short answer

If your vehicle fails emissions, you repair it, and it fails again, Georgia may grant a repair waiver so you can renew your registration. For 2026 registration you need at least $1,176 in qualifying emissions repairs, documented, from a licensed repair facility. Inspection fees, taxes and shop supplies do not count toward it.

What you need to qualify

Your vehicle must fail the initial inspection and then fail again on re-inspection after repairs — two failures, with the repair in between. The initial inspection must be no more than 12 months old. The vehicle cannot be tied to a previous waiver, and the registered owner's address must be in one of the 13 emissions counties.

The 2026 amount, and what counts toward it

The minimum qualifying spend is $1,176 for 2026 registration, up from $1,146 for 2025. It is adjusted annually. Parts and labour both count — but only from a licensed repair facility holding a valid business licence.

What does not count (this catches people out)

Emissions inspection fees. Surcharges. Taxes. Warranty work. Gas. Disposal fees. Shop supplies. Miscellaneous charges. And if you do the repairs yourself, only the parts count — your labour does not. A $1,200 invoice can easily fall short of the threshold once those exclusions come off, so the line items matter.

How we document it

We itemise emissions-related repairs clearly and separately on your invoice, so that what qualifies is obvious to whoever reviews the application. Vague invoicing is the avoidable reason waiver applications get held up.

⚠️ One detail to confirm directly

Some sources describe a repair technician form as part of the waiver process. Georgia's Clean Air Force documentation describes a Repair Waiver Liability Form the motorist signs, and notes that some vehicles require a visual inspection by a certified technician. Before relying on any specific form requirement, call GCAF on 1-800-449-2471 and confirm what your situation needs. We would rather send you to the authority than guess on your behalf.

Common questions

How much do I have to spend for a waiver in 2026?

At least $1,176 in qualifying emissions repairs, from a licensed repair facility. The figure is adjusted every year.

Does the emissions test fee count toward it?

No. Inspection fees, surcharges, taxes, warranty work, disposal fees and shop supplies are all excluded.

Can I count repairs I did myself?

Parts only. Your own labour does not count toward the minimum.

How long does the waiver application take?

Allow around three business days for electronic applications. Applications left incomplete for 30 days are closed.
