Oil Change in Peachtree Corners

What to expect

An oil change here takes about 30 to 45 minutes and includes a genuine multi-point inspection — one where we photograph anything we flag rather than just ticking a box. Most modern engines on full synthetic go 7,500 to 10,000 miles between changes. We follow your manufacturer's interval, not a 3,000-mile sticker.

How often does my car actually need it?

Check the owner's manual or the maintenance minder on the dash. Most vehicles built in the last fifteen years running full synthetic are specified somewhere between 7,500 and 10,000 miles. The 3,000-mile interval is a holdover from conventional oil and older engines. Short trips, towing, and sitting in Atlanta traffic all count as severe service and pull the interval shorter — that is a real thing, not a sales line.

Synthetic or conventional?

If your manufacturer specifies synthetic, that is not a preference, it is a requirement — many modern engines have tight tolerances, turbochargers, or variable valve timing that depend on it. If your vehicle is older and specified for conventional, conventional is fine and cheaper. We will tell you which camp you are in.

What the inspection covers

Fluid levels and condition, belts and hoses, tyre tread depth and pressure, brake pad thickness where visible, battery state of charge, lights, wipers, and anything leaking. Anything we flag gets photographed and sent to you. Nothing is added to the bill without you approving it first.

Common questions

Do you use the right oil for my car?

Yes — we match the viscosity and specification your manufacturer calls for. Using the wrong grade in a modern engine causes real problems, so this is not a place to economise.

Will you try to sell me a flush?

No. Our technicians are salaried, so nobody here earns more by adding services. If your car genuinely needs something, we will photograph the reason.
