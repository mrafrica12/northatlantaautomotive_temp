Engine & Exhaust Repair

What to expect

Engine work is where trust matters most, because the bill is large and you cannot see the problem yourself. That is exactly why we photograph it. Before you approve any engine repair here, you get pictures of the actual failed component and a written explanation of what we found and what it will cost.

Diagnosis before parts

Engine symptoms overlap heavily — a misfire can be ignition, fuel, compression, or a vacuum leak. We test to find which, rather than replacing the cheapest likely part and seeing what happens. That approach costs you less overall even when the diagnostic time is billed.

When we'll tell you not to

Sometimes the honest answer on an engine repair is that the car is not worth it. If the repair approaches or exceeds the vehicle's value, we will say so plainly and help you think it through, even though walking away means we do not get the job.

Exhaust work

Exhaust leaks are a safety issue as well as a noise one, and a leak ahead of the oxygen sensor will upset fuelling and can fail an emissions test. We inspect the whole system rather than just the noisy section.

Common questions

Can you give me a price over the phone?

For a defined job like a timing belt on a known engine, often yes. For a symptom, no — and anyone who does is guessing. We will quote after diagnosis, before any work.
