Brake Repair in Peachtree Corners

What to expect

Most brake jobs at our Peachtree Corners shop are pads and rotors on one axle, and take about two hours. We measure pad thickness and rotor runout, photograph both, and text you the pictures before you approve anything. If your pads still have life in them, we will tell you that instead.

How do I know my brakes actually need doing?

A high-pitched squeal on light braking is usually the wear indicator — a small metal tab designed to make noise before the pad runs out. That is a book-it-this-month noise, not an emergency. Grinding is different: that is metal on metal, it is damaging the rotor every time you stop, and it should be looked at immediately. A pedal that pulses or a steering wheel that shakes under braking usually points at rotor thickness variation rather than pads.

Do I need rotors every time, or is that an upsell?

Not every time. Rotors have a manufacturer minimum thickness stamped into them, and we measure against it. If they are above spec and running true, pads alone are the honest answer. If they are below spec or warped, replacing them is not optional — machining a rotor that is already thin makes it worse. You will see the measurement, not just our word for it.

What we actually do

Road test to confirm the symptom. Wheels off, measure pad thickness and rotor thickness and runout. Inspect calipers, slide pins, hoses and fluid condition. Photograph everything and send it to you. Once you approve, replace what was approved, torque to spec, bed the pads in, and road test again.

Common questions

How long does a brake job take?

About two hours for pads and rotors on one axle, assuming parts are on hand. We will tell you up front if your vehicle needs something we have to order in.

Should I do front and rear at the same time?

Usually not. Front brakes do most of the work and wear roughly twice as fast. Doing all four when only the fronts are worn is a classic way to spend money early for no safety benefit.

Do you use dealer parts?

We use quality parts appropriate to the vehicle, and we will tell you which brand and grade before you approve. If you would rather have OE parts, we will price those too.
