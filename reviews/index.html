Why there is no star rating in this page’s code

Some shops embed review markup so a star rating shows up in search results. On a business that is reviewing itself, that is against Google’s structured data guidelines and can earn a manual penalty. Our rating lives on Google, where it is counted independently. Here it is just text — you can go and check it.
